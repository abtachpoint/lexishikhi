Privacy Policy for LexiShikhi

Last updated: September 8, 2026

LexiShikhi is an English-learning application designed to provide study content, practice materials, and learning tools. We respect your privacy and aim to keep the app simple and privacy-friendly.

Current version summary: LexiShikhi does not require an account and does not collect, store, or sell personally identifiable information through its core learning features.

Information We Collect

The current version of LexiShikhi does not ask users to provide personal information such as name, phone number, address, date of birth, or account credentials.

Local App Data

Some app preferences and progress information may be stored locally on your device. This may include selected language, coin balance, unlocked topics, saved items, and similar app settings. This information is used only to provide app functionality and is not transmitted to our own server.

Advertisements

LexiShikhi does not display automatic advertisements in the current version. If optional rewarded advertisements are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated as necessary before or when that feature is released.

In-App Purchases

The current version does not process purchases through our own systems. If Google Play in-app purchases are enabled in a future version, payment processing will be handled by Google Play. LexiShikhi will not receive your full payment-card details.

Analytics and Tracking

The current version does not include our own analytics or advertising tracking system. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Children's Privacy

LexiShikhi is an educational app. The app does not intentionally collect personal information from children through its core features. If future features change the way data is handled, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Third-Party Services

Some future or platform-level features may rely on third-party services such as Google Play. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Data Security

Because the current version does not operate our own user-account or personal-data server, most app progress information remains on the user's device. Users should protect their devices using appropriate security settings.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if LexiShikhi adds new features, third-party services, advertising, purchases, analytics, or other functionality that changes how information is handled. The updated date at the top of this page will be changed when revisions are made.

Contact

For privacy questions about LexiShikhi, please use the developer contact information shown on the LexiShikhi Google Play Store listing.
